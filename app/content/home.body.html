How we onboard 7 crore

Any MSME with a digital footprint — a website, a listing, a catalogue anywhere online — Aaziko's AI reads it, builds their company profile and product catalogue automatically, and with one click of confirmation they go live: seen by the world, ready to receive orders. The Partnership

Aaziko brings the platform. Government brings the reach.

The platform is built and running. To make 2 crore Indian manufacturers purchasable by the world, we ask government to partner on two things.

1 · Product–Market Fit, cluster by cluster

With the Export Promotion Councils, Chambers of Commerce, ODOP and the MSME Department, Aaziko runs cluster programs that hand each maker the exact market knowledge — which country wants their product, and precisely what to change, at product and packing level, to win it. The maker is already a master of the craft; we give them the market.

2 · Bring the world's buyers to India

Marketing assistance to show global buyers India's products — and the world's easiest way to purchase them: in bulk, with 100% assurance, directly from 2 crore Indian manufacturers. Existing importers and small e-commerce sellers alike, sourcing from India as easily as ordering on Amazon.
