What it does

The single biggest reason international buyers hesitate to order from an unknown Indian manufacturer is simple: they cannot see what is happening to their money. Between payment and delivery sit weeks of silence — and silence kills trust.

Aaziko replaces that silence with evidence. From the moment an order is confirmed, the manufacturer's progress is documented at every production milestone — raw material, production start, mid-production, finishing, packing — with date-stamped photos and videos uploaded to the buyer's order dashboard.

Before anything ships, a third-party inspection is conducted against the order contract — dimensions, quantity, workmanship, packing — using internationally recognised AQL sampling standards. The inspection report goes to the buyer before payment is released through Order Assurance.

This is PM Modi's Zero Defect Zero Effect vision made operational: not a certificate on a wall, but per-order proof of quality that the actual foreign buyer sees. The maker just manufactures — Aaziko organises the evidence and the inspection.
