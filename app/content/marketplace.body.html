How an order flows

One order. Aaziko does the rest.

The buyer places one simple order. The maker only manufactures. Everything in between — contracts, inspection, payment, customs, insurance and global delivery — runs through a single engine. Aaziko is not a middleman: Aaziko executes the entire trade, and carries the risk for both sides.

  1. Buyer places one simple order.
  2. Aaziko signs two contracts — one with the buyer and one with the maker — then sends the manufacturing order to the maker.
  3. Maker manufactures the goods and shares live photos.
  4. Aaziko inspects, pays the maker 100% upfront and takes the goods.
  5. Aaziko handles customs, insurance, freight and logistics.
  6. Delivered to the buyer's door — start to finish, Aaziko carried it.
