What it does

An Indian manufacturer's hardest export question is not "how do I ship?" It is "what should I even make for this market?" Pochampally ikat sells in Tokyo but not Riyadh. UAE buyers want IRAM-certified ceramic tiles; American buyers want LEED-certified ones. Argentinian auto-component buyers reject 10-mm threaded fasteners but accept 12-mm.

Aaziko Product Analysis answers these questions with data. 30 crore data points across 50 countries are continuously processed to surface country-specific demand patterns, price bands, certification requirements, packaging preferences and competitive landscape.

For every Indian product, every export market: what is it worth, who is buying it, at what price, in what volumes, with what certifications, and what specific modifications would convert the next order.

This is the intelligence that turns "I make ceramic tiles" into "I make ceramic tiles in 600×600 mm, glazed finish, IRAM-certified, packed in shrink-wrapped pallets — ready to ship to UAE buyers paying $5.20/sqm."
